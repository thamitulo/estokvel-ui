How the transaction fee works

calculate

Per contribution

The fee is charged each time a member contributes to a stokvel. A R1 000 contribution on the Free plan incurs a R15 fee (3%, capped at R15). On Pro it's R15. On Business, R10.

redeem

Referral credits offset fees

Every successful referral earns you R50 credit. Your friend gets R25 credit on signup. Credits are automatically applied before cash fees are charged — so active referrers often pay nothing.

security

No payout fees

We only charge on contributions — never on payouts. When it's your turn to receive the pool, 100% of the accumulated contributions land in your account.

Common Questions

Can I switch tiers at any time?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle. Switching to Free is always instant.

 What happens to my referral credits if I cancel?

Credits never expire and carry over regardless of your tier. Even on the Free plan, your credits will offset the 3% fee.

 Is VAT included?

All prices shown are VAT-inclusive. eStokvel is registered with SARS and issues valid tax invoices on request.

 What payment methods do you accept?

We currently support EFT (instant EFT via Stitch) and major debit/credit cards. SnapScan and PayShap coming soon.
